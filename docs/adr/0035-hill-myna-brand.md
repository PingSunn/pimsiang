# 0035: The hill myna brand replaces the indigo look

The user compared three directions for PimSiang's identity (the hill myna mascot, a celadon plate and a typewriter) and picked the hill myna, "นกขุนทอง", the Thai bird famous for repeating speech. This supersedes the look of ADR 0034 (indigo accent, waveform logo mark); the sidebar layout, the egui `Style` approach and the verification method from 0034 stay.

The brand kit lives in `docs/brand/` (README, `guidelines.html`, mascot poses, app icon, menu bar glyph, wordmark) and is generated by `docs/brand/source/mascot.py`. Palette: Feather `#1C1A17`, Wattle `#FFD23F`, Beak `#F28C28`, Paper `#FBF7EE`, Live `#E5484D`, Ink-2 `#6B645A`, Rule `#E8E0CF`, Side `#F6F1E4`, Select `#FFE89A`. Titles use Mitr 600 (bundled next to Anuphan, OFL licence kept, fetched by `scripts/fetch-font.sh`); the interface stays Anuphan.

Theme: light is Paper with a Side sidebar, Select for the selected row, and a Feather primary button with Wattle text; switches are Feather with a Wattle knob. Dark uses Feather-dark surfaces (`#16140F`, `#1B1914`, `#1F1C17`) with Wattle accents and selection; the primary button and switches invert to Wattle with a Feather knob or text. Dark mascot variants add a light rim around the feather so the black bird does not vanish.

Rendering the art: the SVGs are rasterised ahead of time to PNG by a small Rust tool on `resvg` (`docs/brand/source/render`, its own workspace, run by `docs/brand/source/render.sh`), and the app embeds the PNGs and decodes them into egui textures with the `png` feature of the `image` crate. The tool also converts Thai letters and the wordmark to paths with Mitr and builds `PimSiang.icns`. The Pill uses 96 px PNG heads as `NSImage`; the menu bar uses 54 px PNG glyphs as template images.

The menu bar glyph is a monochrome template, so state is a small shape at the corner (a dot when listening, three dots when transcribing, a `!` badge for a missing permission) instead of the old grey, red, orange and yellow dots. macOS tints template images itself, and a coloured non-template image would be unreadable on one of the two menu bar appearances.

## Considered options

- **egui_extras with the `svg` feature.** Renders SVG at runtime, but it pulls resvg, usvg and a font database into the shipped binary for nine static pictures, and text in the art would need fonts at runtime. Rejected for build-time rasterisation.
- **NSImage from SVG data in the Pill.** Works on macOS 14 only through a private-ish path and gives no control over rendering at 20 px. Rejected for pre-rendered PNGs.
- **Coloured menu bar icons per state.** Keeps the old colour cues but breaks in one of the two appearances. Rejected.
- **Celadon and typewriter directions.** Not chosen by the user.
